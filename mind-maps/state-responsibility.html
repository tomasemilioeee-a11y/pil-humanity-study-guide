State responsibility

The law of the internationally wrongful act, from attribution and breach through the defences to cessation, reparation, invocation and countermeasures. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Answering a problem question

Work through each act in this order.

  1. Obligation

    Name the primary rule (treaty or custom) and check it bound the State when the act occurred (Art 13).

  2. Attribution

    Organ (Art 4, including de facto organs), governmental authority (Art 5), ultra vires (Art 7), instructions or effective control (Art 8), adoption (Art 11). If none fits, ask about the State's own failure to prevent.

  3. Breach

    Conduct not in conformity with the obligation (Art 12). Is it continuing, composite, or only preparatory?

  4. Defences

    Consent, self-defence, countermeasures, force majeure, distress, necessity (Arts 20–25): test every condition. None for jus cogens (Art 26); compensation may still be due (Art 27).

  5. Consequences

    Cessation and guarantees (Art 30); full reparation: restitution, then compensation, then satisfaction (Arts 34–37); Arts 40–41 for serious breaches of peremptory norms.

  6. Invocation and response

    Who may claim (Art 42 or 48), admissibility (Art 44), and whether countermeasures are lawful (Arts 49–54).
