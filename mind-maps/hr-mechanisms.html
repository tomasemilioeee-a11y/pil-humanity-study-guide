Bringing a complaint

For a problem asking where and how a victim can go.

  1. Choose the forum

    Regional court (binding) or treaty body (views)? Which has the State accepted? Mind the 'same matter' rules.

  2. Standing

    A direct, indirect or potential victim; NGO or association standing; no actio popularis.

  3. Admissibility

    Exhaustion of effective remedies, time limits, ratione personae, materiae, loci and temporis, significant disadvantage.

  4. Urgent protection

    Interim measures (Rule 39) or precautionary measures in the Americas.

  5. Merits

    The substantive right, negative and positive obligations, and any margin of appreciation.

  6. Remedies and follow-up

    Execution under Art 46, the IACtHR's reparations, follow-up procedures, and the case's wider impact.
