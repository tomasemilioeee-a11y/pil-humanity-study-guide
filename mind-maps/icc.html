International criminal justice and the ICC

From Nuremberg to the Rome Statute: the tribunals, the ICC's jurisdiction and triggers, complementarity, immunities and cooperation, withdrawal and pressure, and the alternatives to the Court. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Answering a problem question

For an ICC scenario or an OTP memo.

  1. Jurisdiction

    Subject matter (which crime, with its contextual element), time (Art 11), preconditions (Art 12: territory or nationality), trigger (Art 13).

  2. Preliminary examination

    Reasonable basis, admissibility and the interests of justice (Art 53(1)).

  3. Admissibility

    Any domestic proceedings? If so, same person and conduct, unwilling or unable (Art 17); then gravity.

  4. Getting the accused

    Arrest warrants, cooperation duties, Art 27 versus Art 98, non-compliance (Art 87(7)).

  5. Obstacles

    Withdrawal (Art 127), SC deferral (Art 16), sanctions and politics.

  6. Alternatives

    Domestic universal jurisdiction, special tribunals, State responsibility before the ICJ.
