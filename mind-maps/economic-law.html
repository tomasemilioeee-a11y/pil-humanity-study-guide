Answering a problem question

For a trade measure, then for an investment claim.

  1. Forum

    WTO dispute settlement is compulsory and exclusive (DSU Arts 6.1, 23.1); mention appeals into the void.

  2. Breach

    MFN (Art I), bindings (Art II), national treatment and like products (Art III), quotas and bans (Art XI).

  3. Art XX

    Which paragraph? Necessity or 'relating to'? Then the chapeau: arbitrary or unjustifiable discrimination, disguised restriction.

  4. Art XXI

    An objective emergency in international relations? Security interests articulated in good faith, with a plausible link?

  5. Remedy

    Compliance, then authorised retaliation (DSU Art 22); no unilateral countermeasures (Art 23).

  6. Investment claim

    A protected investor and investment? Which standard was breached (expropriation, FET)? Lawful or unlawful taking, and so which compensation standard?
