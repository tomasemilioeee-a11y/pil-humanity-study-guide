Classifying armed conflict and co-applying IHRL

The first step of every IHL problem: is there an armed conflict, of which kind, and what law follows, including how human rights law applies alongside IHL. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Classifying in a problem question

Do this before applying any substantive rule.

  1. Who fights whom

    Two States, even briefly: an IAC (CA2), including occupation. A State against an armed group, or groups against each other: test for a NIAC.

  2. NIAC test

    Intensity and organisation, with the indicative factors (Haradinaj, Boškoski). Then check whether AP II's higher threshold is met.

  3. Foreign involvement

    A foreign State fighting for the group, or with overall control over it: an IAC between the States (Tadić). For the government: still a NIAC. Consider parallel conflicts.

  4. Applicable law

    Name the instruments for each relationship: GCs and AP I; CA3, AP II and customary IHL; domestic law below the threshold.

  5. Co-apply human rights

    Jurisdiction, derogation, then harmonious interpretation or lex specialis (Nuclear Weapons para 25; Wall para 106; Hassan).

  6. Apply the rules

    Targeting, detention, treatment and occupation rules for each classified relationship.
