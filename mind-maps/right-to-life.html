The right to life in war and peace

When States may kill: the law-enforcement paradigm, planning and investigations, the death penalty, targeting in armed conflict, and targeted killings, including the 2025 boat strikes. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Answering a problem question

For any killing by State agents.

  1. Classify

    Is there an armed conflict (Tadić intensity and organisation)? If not, only the law-enforcement paradigm applies.

  2. Law enforcement test

    A legal framework; force absolutely necessary to protect life from an imminent threat; proportionate; capture impossible.

  3. Planning and control

    Was the operation planned to minimise recourse to lethal force (McCann, Beslan)? Were preventive duties met (Osman)?

  4. If armed conflict

    Who is the target (combatant, direct participant, continuous combat function, hors de combat)? Distinction, proportionality, precautions.

  5. Investigation

    Effective, independent, prompt and transparent; blanket immunity laws breach the right.

  6. Abroad?

    Check jurisdiction for strikes outside the territory (Al-Skeini, Carter, GC 36 para 63).
