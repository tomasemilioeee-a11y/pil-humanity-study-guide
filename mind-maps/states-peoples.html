States and peoples

Who the main subjects of international law are: statehood and its legality, recognition, territory, sovereignty, self-determination and secession. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Answering a problem question

For a secession or statehood question (the Azargh model).

  1. A people?

    Colonial, under alien subjugation or occupation (clear), or a minority inside a State (contested).

  2. What it is entitled to

    Internal self-determination by default; external only for colonial or occupied peoples; remedial secession is contested (Quebec, Kosovo).

  3. Statehood

    Montevideo criteria, strictly applied at birth; effectiveness of government and independence.

  4. Legality

    Born of force or a jus cogens breach? Then other States must not recognise it (Kosovo para 81; ARSIWA Art 41).

  5. Recognition

    Declaratory evidence; premature recognition as intervention; functional statehood as a fallback (Palestine at the ICC).

  6. Territory

    Uti possidetis and title over effectivités; the territorial integrity of the parent State.
