Torture, ill-treatment, starvation and aid

The absolute prohibition of torture and ill-treatment, its definitions, State duties, non-refoulement and rendition, terror in armed conflict, and starvation and humanitarian access. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Answering a problem question

For any allegation of torture or ill-treatment.

  1. Which regime

    Human rights (CAT, ICCPR, ECHR), IHL, or international criminal law, each with its own definition.

  2. Severity and purpose

    Above the minimum level of severity? Torture (severe, purposive) or inhuman or degrading treatment?

  3. Who acted

    Officials, acquiescence, private actors (positive obligations) or a de facto authority?

  4. No justification

    Absolute: no emergency, terrorism or ticking bomb (CAT Art 2(2); Gäfgen).

  5. State duties

    Investigate, prosecute or extradite, redress, exclude torture evidence, no refoulement.

  6. Criminal liability

    Torture as a war crime or crime against humanity; universal jurisdiction.
