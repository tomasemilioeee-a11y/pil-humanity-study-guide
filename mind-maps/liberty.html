Liberty, detention and fair trial

Who may be detained, on what basis and with what review, in peace, in international and non-international armed conflict, and in the 'war on terror', plus the guarantees of a fair trial. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Answering a problem question

The five questions, plus responsibility.

  1. Applicable law

    Classify the situation and identify the human rights treaties and any derogation.

  2. Status

    POW, civilian internee, criminal suspect or someone else, with GCIII Art 5 for doubtful cases.

  3. Authority and grounds

    GCIII Art 21, GCIV Arts 42 and 78, an SC resolution, domestic law, or ECHR Art 5(1)?

  4. Treatment

    Humane treatment, no torture or ill-treatment, conditions.

  5. Review and release

    Review by an independent body, how often, release at the end of hostilities or when grounds lapse.

  6. Responsibility

    Complicity in rendition, habeas corpus, remedies.
