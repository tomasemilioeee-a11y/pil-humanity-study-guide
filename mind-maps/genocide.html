Genocide

The definition and its elements (protected groups, the five acts, intent to destroy in whole or in part), how intent is proved, States' duties to prevent and punish, and The Gambia v Myanmar (19 Nov 2026), the judgment you must cite. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Answering a problem question

For an individual or a State.

  1. Protected group

    National, ethnical, racial or religious, on objective and subjective criteria.

  2. Prohibited act

    Which of Art II(a)–(e), with the specific requirements (e.g. 'calculated' in (c)).

  3. Intent to destroy

    In whole or in a substantial part, as such: physical or biological destruction, not displacement alone.

  4. Inference

    Only reasonable inference from the totality of the evidence; use the para 397 factors and the para 443 refinement.

  5. Responsibility

    Individual: Art III modes, incitement. State: attribution, commission, complicity, failure to prevent (para 487), failure to punish.

  6. Fallback

    If intent fails, crimes against humanity (extermination, persecution) or war crimes.
