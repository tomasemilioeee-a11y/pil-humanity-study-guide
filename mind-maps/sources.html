Proving a customary rule

Use this whenever an answer turns on custom.

  1. State the rule

    Formulate the alleged rule precisely; a vague rule cannot be proved.

  2. Practice

    Collect State practice in all its forms (Concl 6), including specially affected States. Is it widespread, representative and consistent? Is contrary conduct treated as breach?

  3. Opinio juris

    Show acceptance as law separately: statements, treaty practice, GA resolutions handled with care, or induction from extensive practice.

  4. Treaty route

    Did a treaty codify, crystallise or generate the rule (North Sea)? Does it bind non-parties as custom?

  5. Who is bound

    General, regional or bilateral custom? Any persistent objector, and was it consistent from the start? Never against jus cogens.

  6. Rank

    If norms clash, consider jus cogens, Charter Art 103 and lex specialis.
