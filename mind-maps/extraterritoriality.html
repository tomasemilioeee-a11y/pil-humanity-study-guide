Extraterritorial human rights jurisdiction

When people abroad fall 'within the jurisdiction' of a State: the treaty texts, the spatial, personal and impact models, armed conflict before the ECtHR, attribution, and Russia's cases. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Answering a problem question

Test jurisdiction before any substantive right.

  1. Which treaty?

    ECHR Art 1, or ICCPR Art 2(1) read disjunctively; note the bodies' different tests.

  2. Spatial model

    Effective control over the area through forces or a subordinate administration (Loizidou; Ilaşcu).

  3. Personal model

    Custody, public powers, force bringing a person under control, targeted killing (Al-Skeini; Carter).

  4. Impact model

    Under the ICCPR or the American Convention: direct and reasonably foreseeable impact (GC 36; OC-23/17).

  5. Hostilities

    Distinguish Georgia II's 'chaos' from Ukraine and NL v Russia; co-apply IHL.

  6. Then the rights

    Only those relevant to the situation ('divided and tailored'); keep attribution as a separate question.
