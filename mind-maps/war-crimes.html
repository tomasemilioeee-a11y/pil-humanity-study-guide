War crimes

What makes a violation of IHL a war crime: the contextual elements, the Art 8 lists, crimes against persons and in the conduct of hostilities, modes of liability, defences and recent judgments. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

What you must prove

Element by element, for each charge.

  1. Armed conflict

    Show an armed conflict existed when the act occurred (Tadić para 70).

  2. Classification

    IAC or NIAC, to choose the right Art 8 list.

  3. Protected person or object

    A protected person under the GCs, or someone taking no active part; or a protected object.

  4. Nexus

    The conflict played a substantial part in the crime (Kunarac).

  5. The specific crime

    Its actus reus under the Elements of Crimes, and the mental element (Art 30 intent and knowledge).

  6. Liability and defences

    Commission, ordering, aiding, common purpose or command responsibility; then duress, superior orders or mistake.
