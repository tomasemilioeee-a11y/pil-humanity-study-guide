The law of treaties

A treaty's life under the Vienna Convention: what counts as a treaty, how States become bound, reservations, third States, interpretation, invalidity, termination and suspension. Tap any topic for the rule, the authorities and the key quote.

Answering a problem question

Work through the instrument in this order.

  1. Is it a treaty?

    Written, between States, governed by IL, with intent to be bound, whatever its name (Art 2(1)(a); Qatar v Bahrain; Somalia v Kenya).

  2. Is the State bound?

    Authority (Art 7), consent (Arts 11–15), entry into force (Art 24), the interim obligation (Art 18), internal-law objections (Arts 27, 46).

  3. Reservations

    Permissible (Art 19)? Accepted or objected to (Art 20)? Effects (Art 21)? If invalid, severance or no consent?

  4. Meaning

    Art 31 in one go, then Art 32 to confirm; Art 33 for languages; evolutive meaning for generic terms in long-term treaties.

  5. Still in force?

    Termination or suspension grounds (Arts 54–62), followed by the Art 65 procedure. Necessity is not a ground.

  6. Non-performance

    Switch to State responsibility: breach, circumstances precluding wrongfulness, countermeasures (Gabčíkovo para 47).
